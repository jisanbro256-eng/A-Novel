The Eyes Beyond Night

Middle-Zone Outer Perimeter & Village Residence • Nightfall

Natan slowly stood up.

Pain shot through his legs as he tried to move.

His friends quickly supported him, helping him walk out of the arena.

Each step felt heavier than the last.

Outside, the noise of the crowd faded… replaced by a quieter, more personal world.

As they moved through the outer area—

Natan’s eyes landed on her.

Amy.

She stood with her group, speaking confidently, a faint pride in her posture.

Natan walked toward her.

Despite the pain… he smiled.

Natan: “You fought really well.”

Amy glanced at him.

Then rolled her eyes.

Amy: “That’s obvious. You’re not on my level.”

Her group burst into laughter.

From Natan’s side, a girl stepped forward.

Nasha.

Her expression turned sharp.

Nasha: “What a strange person… doesn’t even understand a simple greeting.”

Natan lightly tapped her shoulder.

Natan: “It’s okay. Winning makes people feel proud.”

Nasha froze for a second…

Then slightly blushed.

Nasha: “…Still.”

Natan chuckled softly.

Natan: “It’s okay, Nasha.”

Without saying more, his friends helped him walk away.

Scene Shift – Night

Natan: “Ahh—slow down! That hurts!”

A loud cry echoed inside a warm, cozy house.

Natan lay face down as his mother treated his injuries.

Cold water… cloth… careful hands.

Still, it hurt. A lot.

His mother, Fagui, frowned deeply while applying pressure to his back.

Fagui: “Who told you to fight that girl? Look at you now… what has she done to my son?”

Natan let out a weak chuckle.

Natan: “If I want to get stronger… I have to take some hits.”

Fagui scoffed.

Fagui: “Now you understand? This is what getting hit feels like.”

But despite her words…

Her hands were gentle.

She continued treating him in silence.

Scene Shift – Outside the Village

The night was different.

Too quiet.

Too still.

Beyond the village walls, guards and samurai patrolled the area.

Lanterns flickered in the dark as they scanned their surroundings.

Something felt… wrong.

One samurai stopped.

His grip tightened on his sword.

He saw something.

Ahead… in the darkness.

Two eyes.

Not red. Not blue. Not purple.

Just—

Pure white.

No pupils. No emotion. Just emptiness.

A figure slowly revealed itself.

A samurai… or something wearing one.

Ancient armor. Dark, almost decayed.

A blade rested at its waist.

But the aura around it—

Was wrong. Heavy. Unnatural.

Like the air itself refused to move.

The guard’s legs began to shake.

His mouth opened… but no words came out.

Then—

Realization hit him.

His eyes widened in horror.

He whispered, barely able to breathe—

“…Wait… that means…”
