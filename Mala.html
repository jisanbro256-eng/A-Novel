THE ROYAL TOURNAMENT BEGINS

A New Chapter · A New Battle

✦

Scene 01 — The Tournament

The middle zone was overflowing with people. Visitors had arrived from different regions, filling the streets with excitement and anticipation. Everyone had gathered for one major event—the Royal Tournament.

At the centre of the region stood a massive stadium. Its enormous gates were open, welcoming warriors and spectators alike. The atmosphere was filled with excitement as everyone waited for the tournament to begin.

Inside the stadium, the announcement of the first duel echoed throughout the arena.

FIRST MATCH — FORHAD VS SARA

Both competitors stepped into the arena. The crowd immediately became louder. Forhad stood confidently, while Sara watched him with a focused expression.

Forhad: "Let's make this interesting."

Sara: "Don't underestimate me."

The signal was given. Both warriors moved at once, beginning a fast exchange of attacks. Forhad managed to gain the advantage through his timing and quick reactions, but Sara refused to surrender.

After another intense exchange, Forhad finally secured the victory. The audience erupted with applause.

Sara accepted the result, and the two competitors showed respect for each other before leaving the arena.

✦ ✦ ✦

Scene 02 — The Next Opponents

The excitement inside the stadium continued to rise. The tournament announcer stepped forward and prepared to introduce the next match.

NEXT MATCH — NATAN VS AMY

Two figures entered the arena from opposite sides.

Natan walked forward calmly. He remained silent, showing almost no reaction to the enormous crowd surrounding him.

On the opposite side, Amy approached with a cold and sharp expression. Her eyes remained fixed on Natan.

Neither of them spoke. They simply stood facing each other, carefully observing their next opponent.

The atmosphere changed. The crowd's excitement turned into curiosity as everyone waited for the first move.

Natan slowly raised his gaze.

Amy's expression remained unchanged.

The next battle was about to begin.
