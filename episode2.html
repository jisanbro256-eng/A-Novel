Frozen Resolve

Arena: Middle-Zone Village Stadium • Match #2: Natan vs. Amy

Natan and Amy stepped into the battlefield.

Two different presences.

Two different kinds of cold.

The arena slowly quieted again—the crowd sensing something deeper than just another duel.

They took their positions.

Amy: “Kid… you can still walk away. I won’t blame you if you leave before getting hurt.”

Natan: “Nice words… but overconfidence isn’t a strength.”

Amy: “Then show me what you’ve got.”

A brief silence.

Then—

“FIGHT!”

The announcer’s voice exploded across the stadium.

The ground trembled as both moved instantly.

They clashed. Fast. Clean. Precise.

Every movement was refined—no wasted motion.

Amy struck first, her attacks smooth and controlled, each hit carrying pressure behind it.

Amy: “Nice…”

Natan flipped backward, avoiding a follow-up strike, then suddenly dashed in.

Amy blocked. Countered instantly with a fist aimed at his ribs.

Natan twisted—barely avoiding it—and appeared behind her.

Natan: “Try dodging this.”

A sharp strike landed on Amy’s back. She slid forward slightly.

Amy: “…Nice one. But not enough.”

Her aura changed. The air grew heavier.

Amy stepped forward and unleashed a series of heavy, chained attacks.

Natan blocked some… dodged some… but pressure built quickly.

Natan: “You’re fast…”

Amy didn’t reply. She only attacked harder.

Natan suddenly reached for his kunai. Steel flashed.

Amy responded instantly—drawing her sword.

CLANG! Metal Collided • Sparks Scattered Across Arena

Now it wasn’t just hand-to-hand. It was real combat.

Kill intent started leaking through every movement.

Natan charged forward with kunai strikes. Amy evaded, countered, and forced him back.

The ground beneath them cracked and burned from repeated impacts.

Then—

Natan shifted his stance. A sudden change. Wind gathered around him.

Wind technique.

Amy: “Elemental user…”

She responded immediately. Water energy formed around her blade.

The battlefield changed completely. Wind clashed with water. Fire from earlier residual energy ignited bursts across the field.

Ice began forming at the edges as Amy pushed her technique further.

Steel, wind, water, ice—everything collided. The arena looked like a storm of elements.

Natan pushed forward again, trying to break her defense.

But Amy suddenly changed rhythm. A feint. A distraction.

For a split second—Natan hesitated.

That was enough.

Amy’s aura sharpened. A hidden technique activated. Leaf poison energy spread silently through the air.

Natan’s movement slowed. His breathing turned uneven.

Natan: “Tch… poison…?”

Amy didn’t answer. She stepped in. Final stance. One decisive strike.

BANG!

Natan was hit directly. He flew backward and crashed onto the ground. Dust rose around him.

Silence.

The referee stepped in.

Referee: “One… two… three…”

“AMY WINS!”

The crowd erupted.

Amy stood still for a moment… then turned away without celebration. No smile. No emotion. Just cold victory.

Amy: “Told you.”

She walked off the field. She didn’t even look back.

Natan tried to stand, struggling. His friends rushed in.

Friends: “Are you okay?”

Natan: “…Yeah… I’m fine.”

But his eyes said something different.

He wasn’t just hurt. He was thinking. Watching. Learning.
