The Royal Tournament Begins

Arena: Middle-Zone Village Stadium • Status: Active Duels

The middle-zone village was alive with chaos.

Crowds poured in from every corner—merchants, travelers, fighters, and nobles—filling every street and alley. The village, normally quiet and forgotten, had transformed into a roaring center of excitement.

At its heart stood the Royal Tournament Stadium—a massive arena surrounded by stone seats rising like a colosseum. Almost a year had passed since the last tournament… and now, the world had gathered once again.

The air itself felt heavy with anticipation. Inside the stadium, thousands of voices blended into one endless noise.

“Welcome, everyone… to the Royal Tournament!”

The crowd erupted into a thunderous roar.

“Today marks the 5th day of the tournament! Only three duels remain!”

A pause… then the announcer’s voice grew louder, shaking the banners hanging from the high arches.

“Our first duel of the day… FORHAD VERSUS SARA!”

The roar of the crowd shook the stone foundations. From opposite heavy iron gates, two figures stepped forward into the sun-scorched dust. Silence fell for a brief, tense moment.

Forhad

Stood calm, eyes sharp but relaxed—pure confidence hidden behind absolute silence.

Sara

Stepped in with a cold expression, gaze unreadable—like a storm locked behind solid ice.

“Get set… ready… FIGHT!”

The ground cracked as both of them moved instantly.

Forhad dashed forward—fast, precise. Sara met him without a single second of hesitation.

BAM! Fists Collided Mid-Air • Shockwave Unleashed

Sara twisted backward, activating a wind-imbued technique that pushed her movement far beyond human limits. She struck—fast and sharp—but Forhad flipped away, dodging cleanly through the air. He landed and immediately hurled kunai-like projectiles.

Sara evaded—barely. In a blink, Forhad closed the distance again. A heavy kick slammed directly into Sara’s guard, forcing her backward as she skidded across the cracked stone ground.

Her eyes didn’t change. Cold. Focused. She stepped forward again. Punches. Blocks. Counterattacks. A brutal exchange of pure martial skill.

Then—Sara slammed her fist into the ground. CRACK—!!!

The arena floor shattered beneath them. Forhad staggered for a split second—and that was enough. Sara moved in, a lethal spinning kick aimed straight for his head.

Impact! Forhad flew back, but recovered mid-air, flipping once… twice… and landed low on one knee. The crowd held its breath.

Forhad slowly stood up. Then he unleashed a flurry of strikes—faster than before. Sara tried to defend, but several hits broke through her guard, forcing her back. Spotting a final opening, Forhad stepped in and delivered a decisive kick.

Sara hit the ground hard. Silence fell over the stadium.

“One… two… three! FORHAD WINS!”

The stadium exploded into chaotic cheers. Forhad exhaled slowly, walking toward Sara and extending his hand. She looked at it for a moment, then accepted it.

Sara: “You’re strong… your hits are effective.”

Forhad: “You’re not weak either.”

Forhad: “You know the next fight, right?”

Sara: “…I almost forgot. That one’s going to be intense.”

They both looked toward the gates. Something heavier was coming.

“Next fight… the one everyone has been waiting for! Two students… both known for their terrifying potential!”

“WELCOME… NATAN AND AMY!”
